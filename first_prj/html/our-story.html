🏍 OUR STORY

Born from
Speed &
Passion.

Velox was founded by riders, for riders. We exist to bring the raw power of Kawasaki machines to every road, trail, and waterway.

HOW IT STARTED
The Velox Journey
2018

The Beginning

Velox was born in a small garage with one mission: make premium Kawasaki vehicles accessible to every passionate rider. Our founders — lifelong motorsport enthusiasts — saw a gap in the market and decided to fill it.

2020

First Dealership

We opened our first official showroom, partnering directly with Kawasaki to offer the full lineup — from the iconic Ninja series to the unstoppable Mule and Teryx off-road machines.

2022

Going Online

We launched the Velox platform to bring the showroom experience directly to you. Browse, configure, and order your Kawasaki from anywhere — backed by our expert team.

2026

Today

Thousands of happy customers, a growing dealer network, and an unshakable commitment to speed, quality, and after-sales service. This is only the beginning.
