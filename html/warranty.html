WHAT WE COVER
Full protection details
🔧

Engine

Complete engine internals, pistons, crankshaft, camshaft and all factory components.

⚡

Electronics

ECU, wiring harness, sensors, ignition system and all factory electrical parts.

🛞

Drivetrain

Transmission, clutch, driveshaft, differential and all power transfer components.

🛡️

Brakes

ABS module, brake calipers, master cylinders and hydraulic brake systems.

🔩

Suspension

Front forks, rear shock, linkages and all suspension-related components.

🧊

Cooling

Radiator, water pump, thermostat, hoses and complete cooling circuit.

Not covered

  • Normal wear items (tires, brake pads, chains)
  • Damage from accidents or misuse
  • Modifications or aftermarket parts
  • Neglected maintenance
  • Cosmetic scratches (Basic plan)
  • Racing or track use damage
  • Water damage from improper use
  • Consumables (oil, filters, fluids)
